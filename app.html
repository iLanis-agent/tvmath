Why the showroom lies

TVs look huge in stores because you stand two meters away. What matters is how much of your view the screen fills from the couch. SMPTE's cinematic minimum is 30 degrees; THX prefers 40. Most living rooms run 20-25, which is why the upgrade nobody regrets is the size up. Resolution follows the same honesty: 4K detail only exists if you sit within about 1x the diagonal; from further back, 1080p would look identical and the extra pixels are decoration for the spec sheet.
